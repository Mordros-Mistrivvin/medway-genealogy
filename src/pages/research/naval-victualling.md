# Matthew Medway and naval victualling

## Conclusion

The 1781 court recognizance directly describes **Matthew Meadway of Hamworthy as a victualler**. That is an occupational fact. It is **not evidence by itself that he supplied the Royal Navy or worked for the Victualling Board**.

The recovered Hill case material concerns money belonging to Matthew: eight guineas in gold, two dollars in silver, one silver crown piece and three brass half-crown pieces. The coin list does not establish a naval payment, supply transaction or level of wealth. Matthew appears as victim/prosecution witness, not defendant.

## Identity questions must stay separate

| Proposition | Assessment |
|---|---|
| A Matthew Meadway was associated with Hamworthy in 1781 | **Established** |
| He was described as a victualler | **Established** |
| The Hill case concerned his money | **Established** |
| He supplied naval ships | **Unproved** |
| He was the Matthew who married Mary Galley in 1786 | **Strong identity question, still unproved** |
| He was the West Lulworth child baptised in 1758 | **Unproved** |

The 1758 candidate would have been about 22 in July 1781, so the chronology is possible. Age compatibility is not an identity proof.

Start with **ADM 112/167** and inspect any contemporary index before reading entries. Search Medway/Meadway variants, Poole/Hamworthy, partners and business names. If no relevant entry appears, record exactly what was checked before moving to 1780 and 1782.

Other possible series are **ADM 111/85–88** (Victualling Board minutes around 1781), **ADM 224/60, /62 and /80** if employment becomes plausible, and Royal Museums Greenwich **ADM/C/641–644**, correspondence covering 1781. These are research leads, not evidence that Matthew appears in them.

## Search results so far

Targeted National Archives and Royal Museums Greenwich catalogue searches found **no eighteenth-century exact-name hit that identifies Matthew of Hamworthy as a naval contractor**. Many search results concern the ship or place Medway. Negative catalogue searches do not exclude an entry in an unindexed volume.

## Separate naval household

Two Greenwich Hospital School catalogue entries are genuinely relevant to the surname but currently separate from Matthew:

- **ADM 73/294/54** — John Allen Medway/Meadway, admitted 27 April 1796; parents John and Mary; baptism 21 May 1783 at Stoke Damerel, Devon; father said to be serving in the Royal Navy.
- **ADM 73/294/34** — John Allen Meadway, bond dated 23 May 1796, again naming parents John and Mary.

There is **no established relationship** between this household and Matthew of Hamworthy.

## Better local route to identity

Before assuming a naval occupation, the most promising work is local: identify the full archive reference for the 1781 Hill proceedings and search Hamworthy licensing, leases, rates, settlement papers and recognizances. A named inn or premises, wife, surety or previous residence could connect the 1781 victualler to the 1786 groom — or distinguish a different man.

# The direct line: what is currently secure

The public site concentrates on the historical evidence and deliberately withholds dates, certificates and other identifying details for living or potentially living relatives.

## Direct line carried by the project

The project's working tree runs from the present-day family through the Southampton and Hamworthy generations to:

- **John Medway**, baptised at Hamworthy on **22 May 1854**, son of John and Ann; his father is described as a shipwright.
- **John Medway**, born about 1825 at Hamworthy, who married **Ann Isabella Stokes** at Hamworthy on **7 September 1847**. The marriage identifies his father as **James Medway, shipwright**.
- **James Medway**, baptised at Poole St James on **19 August 1800**, son of **Matthew and Mary Medway**. James married **Harriet Musselwhite** at Lytchett Minster on **26 September 1824** and died young in 1827.
- **Matthew Medway and Mary Galley**, married at Poole St James on **13 August 1786**.

The modern Southampton steps above James are treated as established in the project's working tree from parish and civil material, but the major 9 September audit did not independently re-check every nineteenth- and twentieth-century certificate. Those records should be added to this site individually as the source catalogue grows.

## The anchor at 1800

The original Poole register records **James, son of Matthew and Mary Medway, baptised 19 August 1800**. That entry is the secure historical anchor from which the current research works backwards.

The marriage of his parents is also secure: **Matthew Medway, “Sojourner Batchelor”, and Mary Galley, “of this Parish Spinster”, married 13 August 1786 at Poole St James**. The wording proves their marital status and parish description at marriage; it does not state either person's parents or birthplace.

## Matthew's origin remains the break point

The strongest current origin candidate is **Matthew, son of John and Elizabeth Medway, baptised at West Lulworth on 8 November 1758**. That baptism is genuine. The missing proof is identity: no recovered record yet explicitly carries that child from West Lulworth into the adult Matthew found at Hamworthy and Poole.

The age evidence is contradictory. In 1841 Matthew and Mary were both recorded as 85. Matthew's Hamworthy burial on 21 February 1849 records him as **98**, implying about 1750–51 if accurate. Neither age should be altered to make the 1758 baptism fit.

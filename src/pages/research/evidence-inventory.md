# Evidence inventory

The 9 September audit recovered **429 distinct image files after exact-byte deduplication**. Of those, 417 decoded successfully and 12 did not. Fifty image IDs received documented targeted close examination. This page publishes the most consequential examined items; it is not a claim that each crop or duplicate is an independent source.

Original image files containing private or potentially living-person information are not automatically republished on this public site.

## Non-image supporting records

- Irish civil birth register, page 396 entry 309: **Margaret Susan O'Neill**, born 4 June 1921, parents John O'Neill and Mary formerly Hagan. It identifies that child and parents, not the proposed later relationship.
- Local-history `Name-Index-combined-0.doc`: useful later Southampton search pointers, but derivative; underlying records must be retrieved.
- Earlier archival research strategy: source of catalogue leads, not evidence that every listed record has been inspected.

## Audit principle

An overview inspection is not a full transcription. A crop is not a second source. A catalogue description is not the original deed. A transcription can corroborate a reading, but the original remains controlling where available.

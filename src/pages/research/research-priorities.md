# Research priorities and archive references

The best next record is not necessarily the oldest one. These priorities are ordered by how likely a record is to **distinguish between competing identities or bridge an otherwise unproved relationship**.

## Start here

| Priority | Record / archive reference | Question | What would change the case |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Matthew Medway's **1849 civil death registration** | Does it corroborate the burial age 98 and name a useful informant? | Informant, age and address; agreement may share the same informant and is not automatically independent |

## Mary Galley / Gally priorities

1. **D-DAS/4434A and D-DAS/4434B**, 8 February 1749 — obtain complete lease/counterpart, endorsements and seals. The catalogue calls William Gally of Hamworthy a “relation” of William Gally junior; only the document can show whether any fuller kinship wording survives.
2. **D-DAS/4462**, 7 May 1753 — determine exactly how William Gally is recited in the later Rogers lease.
3. **D-DAS/4523**, 20 December 1766 — determine whether William is a surviving life, former tenant or merely part of title history.
4. Reconstruct **John Galley of Hamworthy**, especially settlement/apprenticeship, marriage, militia, children and death.
5. Retrieve the original **Mary Medway burial, 22 July 1842, age 88**, and assess the age against the 1760 candidate without assuming it is exact.

## Naval-victualling test

The occupational word “victualler” does not prove naval supply. If the hypothesis is pursued, inspect in this order:

- **ADM 112/167** — Victualling Office Contract Ledger, 1781.
- **ADM 112/166** and **ADM 112/168** — adjacent 1780 and 1782 ledgers.
- **ADM 111/85–88** — Victualling Board minute books covering 1781.
- **ADM 224/60, ADM 224/62, ADM 224/80** — establishment/pay records only if employment becomes plausible.
- Royal Museums Greenwich **ADM/C/641–644** — 1781 Admiralty correspondence; ask whether **ADM/G** provides an index/finding route.
- Separate collateral household: **ADM 73/294/54** and **ADM 73/294/34**, John Allen Medway/Meadway, 1796. These records currently have **no established relationship to Matthew**.

## Other catalogue leads retained from earlier work

The earlier research strategy identified **D/WLC M74** (1640 survey), **D/WLC M98** (1687 court material), **D/WLC M91** (1721 rental), other Lulworth court sequences, **PE/WLU/OV/2/1**, Poole apprenticeship material, and **D-FLO/2890**. They remain leads, not records claimed as inspected. Their present catalogue descriptions and ordering conditions should be verified before a visit or copy request.

## New collateral lead: Cattistock Richard Meadway

The 1780–1782 Cattistock land-tax sheets establish a Richard Meadway there but provide no relationship to the direct line. The next useful step is to identify the **formal archive series/shelfmark**, then search Cattistock parish, settlement, probate and land records for an occupation, family or place of origin.

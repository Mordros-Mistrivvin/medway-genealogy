# Earlier Dorset Medways and Meadways

This page deliberately separates **records that exist** from **relationships that remain hypotheses**. There is not yet a proved continuous pedigree from Matthew Medway of Hamworthy back through West Lulworth, Herston and the seventeenth-century clergy family.

## West Lulworth: the eighteenth-century problem

The West Lulworth register contains **Matthew, son of John and Elizabeth Medway, baptised 8 November 1758**. He remains the leading origin candidate for the later Matthew of Hamworthy because the name, geography and chronology are plausible. The identification is **not proved**, and Matthew's Hamworthy burial age of 98 in 1849 points instead to about 1750–51 if accurate.

Several men called John must be kept separate:

| Record | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Swanage baptism, 20 Mar 1710/11 | John, son of John and Mary Medway | That he is the 1734 groom |
| Swanage burial, 23 Nov 1721 | A John Medway of West Lulworth died | Which earlier John he was |
| Pimperne marriage, 17 May 1734 | John Medway married Elizabeth Meaden | His parents or previous residence |
| Church Knowle baptism, 24 Jul 1734 | John, son of John and Elizabeth Meadway | That the parents are certainly the Pimperne couple |
| East Stoke marriage, Jan 1754 | John Medway of West Lulworth married Dorothy Meech of Ower | His identity with any other John; exact day remains 7/17 in dispute |
| Settlement examination, 24 Apr 1762 | John Medway of West Lulworth was a **yeoman** employing William Stibbs | Kinship to Stibbs or Matthew |
| William Bartlet will, 1762/63 | **John Meadway senior**, friend, executor and residuary beneficiary; commission describes him as West Lulworth yeoman | That “senior” identifies his father or son |

The **William Bartlet probate packet 1763/24** is a priority because a complete packet or subsequent property record may identify family, land or signatures.

## Herston: a documented family, an unproved bridge

A catalogue description for **D-BOC/UA/Accession889/Box13/E6**, dated **18 November 1702**, names **Matthew Medway of Herston, yeoman**, his **son and heir John**, and his **daughter Martha**. The associated property includes a Swanage house, interests in the George at Corfe Castle and a Fitzworth holding. **D-RWR/T/78** contains a later copy of the premarital settlement involving John Dolling and Martha Medway. These may represent the same underlying transaction and must not be counted as two independent proofs.

A coherent candidate household surrounds that settlement:

- Mathewe Medway married **An Hellier** at Swanage, 2 October 1665.
- Mary, daughter of Matthew, was baptised at Coombe Keynes, 3 July 1667.
- John, son of Mathew and Anne, was baptised 6 February 1675/6 according to the parish transcription; the supplied original is too faint for a complete independent reading.
- George Medway was buried at Swanage on 14 December 1700.
- A published PCC administration index records **George Meadway of Swanage**, administration 18 January 1700/1, with **Matthew his father**; the original act is still required.
- John Dolling married Martha Medway on 7 January 1702/3.

**Open hypothesis:** the adult Matthew of Herston may be **Matthias**, baptised at Coombe Keynes on 2 February 1639/40, son of Richard the clerk/curate. Chronology permits it; no recovered record yet states the relationship.

## Richard the clerk and the Christopher family

Thomas Christopher's registered PCC will, **PROB 11/219, page 280**, explicitly names his daughter **Pascue Midway**, the **relict of Richard Midway, clerk**. This securely proves Pascue/Pasche's father and husband as stated in that will.

The same will names **Thomas Christopher of Andover**, **William Christopher of London**, wife Joan and daughter Frances. Thomas of Andover is a genuine Hampshire connection in the maternal family; it does not prove that Matthias lived with him.

Coombe Keynes material records children associated with Richard's household: **Christopher (1632), Katherine (1634), Theophilus (1636), Matthias (1639/40) and Mary (1642)**. Richard was buried in September 1643. Theophilus was buried 28 October 1649, so he should not be given a speculative adult career. Thomas Christopher was buried 10 October 1651 and is described in the original as **late keeper of the park in East Lulworth**, not “trooper”.

The published Oxford alumni entry describes Richard as **son of Richard, of Askerswell**, matriculated at Hart Hall on 24 November 1626 aged 23. CCEd records support ordination as deacon in 1628 and priest in 1631, with Bristol subscription-book reference **EP/A/10/1/2**. These identify a clerical chronology, not a specific mother or Askerswell baptism.

## Askerswell: correct the 1560 starting point

The original Askerswell marriage entry of **27 October 1560 is Thomas Meadwaye and Anna**, not Richard and Anna. Any pedigree built on “Richard Meadway married Anna in 1560” must therefore be rejected and rebuilt.

Other early Askerswell records establish surname presence but not direct descent, including **Richard Meadway buried 28 March 1613**, **Robert Meadway buried 1 January 1625**, **Rogerus Meadway baptised 1592, son of Robert and Katerine**, and **Joanna Meadway baptised 1594, daughter of Robert and Katerine**. The early register has displaced leaves, so page order alone is unsafe. A secure original baptism for the clergyman Richard around 1602–03 has not yet been recovered.

## Powerstock and related records

Powerstock registers provide further early Meadway anchors: **Thomas son of Richard Meadway, baptised 4 January 1615/16**; **Alexander son of Thomas, baptised 26 February 1642**; **John Meadway, baptised 3 October 1652**; and **Elles/Ellis Meadway, baptised 12 March 1578/79, father William**. These demonstrate a substantial surname network in west Dorset. They do **not** by themselves connect to the direct Hamworthy line.

Probate and court records also require separation:

- **Thomas Meadway of Powerstock**, administration 14 December 1668: John Goodinge, miller, administrator; William Olliffe, joiner of Blandford, surety. Their roles do not prove kinship.
- **Richard Meadway “the elder”**, Quarter Sessions 1689, ordered to account for militia money: a late-seventeenth-century adult, not Richard the curate who died in 1643.
- **Richard Medway of Burton Bradstock**, dead by the 1691 administration in which Grace is his relict.
- **Richard Medway of Litton Cheney**, alive to make a will on 30 March 1692: necessarily a different Richard from the man dead in 1691.
- **Grace Medway**, 1695 administration with a nuncupative will annexed; John Foster is the preferred reading for principal legatee/administrator, but the relationship is not stated.

## Connections that must remain open

1. Hamworthy Matthew = West Lulworth Matthew baptised 1758.
2. John and Elizabeth of the West Lulworth household = the Pimperne couple married 1734.
3. The 1734 groom = John baptised at Swanage in 1710/11.
4. His father = the Coombe Keynes John baptised 1675/6 and/or the 1702 Herston son and heir.
5. Matthew of Herston = Matthias, son of Richard the curate, baptised 1639/40.
6. Richard the curate = son of a particular Askerswell household reconstructed from the parish registers.

Each proposition needs its own bridge. Proving one does not automatically prove the next.

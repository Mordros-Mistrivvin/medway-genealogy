# Medway Genealogy Research

Evidence-led public research website for the Medway / Meadway family-history project, with particular attention to Southampton, Hamworthy, Poole, Lytchett Minster, Purbeck, Herston, Coombe Keynes, Askerswell and Powerstock.

Live site: **https://mordros-mistrivvin.github.io/medway-genealogy/**

## Research principles

The site distinguishes facts directly stated by records from candidate identities and open hypotheses. Original registers, wills, court papers, leases and other contemporary sources take priority over catalogue descriptions, transcriptions, indexes and user-created family trees. Corrections remain visible rather than being silently overwritten.

The public site deliberately limits identifying details for living or potentially living relatives.

## Main pages

- `/research/latest-findings/` — findings from 10–12 September 2026, including the 1749 Gally lease, Mary Cole probate, Cattistock land tax and naval-victualling work.
- `/research/project-review/` — consolidated project evidence review, corrections, exclusions and unresolved links.
- `/research/direct-line/` — current documented line through James Medway, baptised 1800.
- `/research/mary-galley/` — full Mary Galley / Lytchett–Hamworthy case, including `D-DAS/4434A/B`, `D-DAS/4462` and `D-DAS/4523`.
- `/research/early-line/` — West Lulworth, Herston, Christopher/clergy, Askerswell, Powerstock and the multiple Richards.
- `/research/evidence-inventory/` — audit register of the principal closely examined primary-source images.
- `/research/naval-victualling/` — test of the 1781 “victualler” description against Admiralty/Victualling Board records.
- `/research/cattistock-land-tax/` — Richard Meadway in Cattistock assessments, 1780–1782.
- `/research/research-priorities/` — prioritised archive plan and shelfmarks.
- `/research/method/` — evidence grades, recovery coverage, corrections and privacy policy.
